Our Core Values

The Pillars of NP Wardrobe

Conscious luxury tailored for modern lifestyle

01

Himalayan Craftsmanship & Ethics

We honor centuries of South Asian garment architecture by partnering directly with seasoned master artisans in Patan and Kathmandu, ensuring fair living wages and preserving heritage needlework.

02

Inclusive & True Nepali Sizing

Say goodbye to awkward foreign fast-fashion proportions. Every piece is sculpted specifically for Nepali torso curves, heights, and draping needs, offering confidence from XS to Custom Plus.

03

Personal WhatsApp Concierge

Experience bespoke shopping from your couch. Our Kathmandu styling team offers 1-on-1 video previews of fabrics, size measurements guidance, and tailored color recommendations within minutes.
