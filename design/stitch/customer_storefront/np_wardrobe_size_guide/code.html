Wrap the measuring tape across the fullest part of your bust line with your regular everyday bra on. Keep tape level across shoulder blades.

Locate the narrowest part of your torso (typically 1 to 1.5 inches above your belly button). Breathe out naturally and measure without cinching.

03

3. Widest Hips

Stand naturally with your feet together. Measure around the widest part of your hips and seat (roughly 7-8 inches below your natural waistline).
