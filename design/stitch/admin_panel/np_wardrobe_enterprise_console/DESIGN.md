## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an administrative interface for a high-end luxury fashion brand based in Nepal. It strikes an intentional balance between **editorial haute couture** and **rigorous enterprise operational utility**.

### Target Audience & Persona
- **Operations Managers & Merchandisers**: High-frequency users processing multi-sku orders, seasonal capsule drops, real-time inventory adjustments, and courier dispatch across Nepal’s 7 provinces.
- **Finance & Audit Controllers**: Specialists verifying manual and digital payments (eSewa, Khalti, direct bank transfers, and cash-on-delivery reconciliations) requiring tamper-evident logs and unequivocal data status.

### Emotional Response & Principles
- **Uncompromising Precision**: No speculative or ambiguous UI. Data tables, status tags, and actions are explicit, deterministic, and audit-backed.
- **Sartorial Restraint**: Elevated above standard cold blue SaaS platforms by using warm stone undertones, crisp hairline dividers, and an iconic deep crimson focal point directly derived from the brand mark.
- **Clarity Over Clutter**: Generous tabular density with optical hierarchy. Actions are distinct, irreversible steps trigger explicit modals, and status badges communicate operational truth immediately.

### Stylistic Movement
A blend of **Modern Minimalist SaaS** and **Editorial Luxury**:
- Pure flat geometry with sharp 1px structural framing.
- High-density layouts optimized for 1440px and 1280px operational consoles.
- Subdued, warm neutrals creating an ivory canvas that lets garment photography, color-coded status chips, and typography carry the visual hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for administrative reliability without sacrificing the brand’s deep crimson identity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#B3192B`)**: Derived directly from the brand insignia. Used sparingly for primary interactive buttons, active navigation markers, destructive confirmation accents, and key indicator pins. It is never applied indiscriminately across whole backgrounds.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#44403C`) & Primary Text (`#1C1917`)**: Warm stone charcoal tones that replace generic harsh `#000000`. Ensures optimal contrast on long auditing sessions while preserving a tactile, warm paper feel.
- **Surfaces**: Canvas begins at `#FAFAF9` (warm stone ivory). Structured data panels and cards utilize `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#E7E5E4` hairline strokes to eliminate visual vibration.
- **Status Decoupling Rule**: The system strictly separates **Order Lifecycle** from **Payment Lifecycle**. Green is exclusively reserved for audited, verified events (e.g., confirmed bank reconciliation, completed delivery). An order marked "Confirmed" or having an uploaded receipt voucher will never display an automatic green badge.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes legibility, scannability, and numeric precision across high-density tabular workspaces.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers an elevated modern geometry for administrative page headers, card titles, and modal headers without consuming excessive vertical space.
- **Data & Body (Inter)**: Employs `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` across all tables, financial summaries, and inventory levels to guarantee perfectly aligned tabular numerals.
- **Currency & Identifiers**: Nepali Rupee amounts must consistently format with the prefix `रु.` followed by standard comma grouping (e.g., `रु. 14,500.00`). SKUs, order tokens (e.g., `#NPW-89412`), and carrier tracking codes leverage consistent 13px tabular body weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The administrative architecture is constructed on a **dense fluid-grid workbench model** bounded by structural constraints for multi-window desktop operations.

### Layout Geometry
- **Primary Shell**: Fixed vertical navigation sidebar (260px expanded, 72px icon-collapsed) paired with a persistent 60px header containing omnibar search, branch/warehouse context, and notification triggers.
- **Main Workspace**: Stretches across the remaining viewport with a maximum content canvas of 1600px, centering gently on ultra-wide monitors.
- **Density Grid**: Internal card and table cells utilize tight 8px-multiple vertical padding (`py-2.5` to `py-3`) and horizontal padding (`px-4`) to ensure at least 15 visible line items per standard 1080p screen without premature scrolling.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: Full persistent sidebar, split-pane audit views (order details + verification panel side-by-side).
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: Sidebar collapses into an off-canvas drawer; secondary inspectors switch to bottom sheets or stacked cards.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Tables reflow into dedicated operational cards featuring prioritized badge ribbons and swipeable action menus.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects heavy skeuomorphism and excessive drop shadows in favor of **structural 1px surface boundaries and restrained ambient depth**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FAFAF9` raw ground. All persistent layout shells and canvas regions reside here.
- **Level 1 (Surface Containers & Tables)**: `#FFFFFF` panels bounded by solid `1px` borders (`#E7E5E4`). No shadow required; edge separation is achieved purely via tonal contrast.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Contextual Popovers)**: Dropdown menus, combobox options, and hover cards utilize `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)` with a `1px` border (`#D6D3D1`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Drawers & Detail Inspectors)**: Side-drawers for audit timelines and payment screenshot comparisons utilize `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)` alongside a neutral warm backdrop tint `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.45)`.
- **Level 4 (Critical System Alerts & Toasts)**: Positioned at viewport top/bottom with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Soft / Tailored** curvature (`roundedness: 1`), embodying tailored garment cuts rather than playful software bubbles.

### Dimensional Standards
- **Buttons, Inputs & Dropdowns**: `0.375rem` (`rounded-md`, 6px). Retains sharp professional posture while avoiding harsh 90-degree corners.
- **Cards & Data Tables**: `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`, 8px) with internal table cells having zero radius to maintain clean gridlines.
- **Status Badges & Pills**: `0.25rem` (`rounded`, 4px) or complete pill (`9999px`) strictly for numeric counters and verification tags.
- **Avatars & Color Swatches**: Square with soft corners (`rounded-md`, 6px) for fabric/product thumbnails; circular (`rounded-full`) for admin user profiles.

## Components

Components follow strict Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui patterns tailored for operational speed.

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#B3192B`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, hover `#941423`. Used solely for primary view submissions (e.g., "Confirm Order", "Save Capsule Drop").
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, text `#1C1917`, hover `#F5F5F4`. Default for utility actions, filters, and exports.
- **Destructive**: Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FCA5A5`, text `#B91C1C`, hover `#FEE2E2`. Used for "Reject Payment", "Cancel Order", "Void Waybill".

### 2. Status & Payment Badges
- Built as high-visibility inline tags using uppercase or title-case text (`text-[11px] font-semibold tracking-wide py-0.5 px-2 rounded`).
- **Order Lifecycle**:
  - `Pending`: Amber soft (`#FEF3C7` / `#92400E`)
  - `Confirmed`: Blue soft (`#EFF6FF` / `#1E40AF`)
  - `Processing`: Purple soft (`#F5F3FF` / `#5B21B6`)
  - `Shipped`: Cyan soft (`#ECFEFF` / `#155E75`)
  - `Delivered`: Emerald soft (`#ECFDF5` / `#065F46`)
  - `Cancelled`: Rose soft (`#FEE2E2` / `#991B1B`)
- **Payment Verification**:
  - `Unpaid`: Stone soft (`#F5F5F4` / `#44403C`)
  - `Pending Verification`: Yellow/Amber warning (`#FFFBEB` / `#B45309`) with an alert dot
  - `Paid`: Forest emerald (`#DCFCE7` / `#166534`)
  - `Rejected / Failed`: Crimson outline (`#FEF2F2` / `#B91C1C`)
  - `Refunded`: Slate soft (`#F1F5F9` / `#334155`)

### 3. Data Tables
- Integrated column sorting, sticky headers with light border separation (`border-b border-stone-200`), row hover highlights (`hover:bg-stone-50/80`), and checkbox row-selection controls.
- Numeric columns (Price, Stock On Hand, Reserved) align right with tabular figures.

### 4. Input Fields & Dropdowns
- Height standardized to 36px (`h-9`) for compact density.
- Border `1px solid #E7E5E4`, focus ring `2px solid #B3192B` with zero pixel offset.
- Form helper text positioned cleanly below fields using 12px muted stone.

### 5. Document & Payment Voucher Inspector
- Split drawer component featuring dual panes: customer-uploaded transfer slip (with zoom/rotate controls) on the left, and transaction metadata fields (reference ID, bank code, amount match indicator) on the right.
